# Galalaxy icon production prompt

Mode: ImageGen image generation with reference images.

References:

- The actual white player-ship sprite for its silhouette and visual identity.
- The actual Galalaxy title screen for its cyan, magenta and deep-space palette.

Prompt:

> Create a new premium 1024×1024 store icon for the arcade space roguelite
> **Galalaxy**. Preserve the recognizable top-down silhouette and white hull
> design of the tiny player-ship reference, but render it as polished
> high-resolution game key art with smooth clean contours, fine metal panel
> detail, glossy highlights and sharp antialiased edges. Absolutely no pixel-art
> treatment, blocky pixels, mosaic texture or low-resolution aesthetic. Center
> one heroic white ship flying upward toward a luminous cyan-and-magenta cosmic
> vortex. Show vivid orange-blue engine exhaust, restrained electric energy
> trails, deep navy space and a few small stars. Strong thumbnail readability,
> symmetric composition, large ship, high contrast and cinematic lighting while
> remaining faithful to the game's neon palette. Keep all important content
> inside the central 80% safe area. No text, logo, border, UI, other ships or
> watermark.

The generated 1024×1024 master was then center-cropped to 5:4 and resized to
1000×800 for the Kongregate upload asset.
